Sushi Bento — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-15 · Publisher: Dirty Leds Games · App: com.dirtyleds.sushisquares

Summary

Sushi Bento is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts and no sign-in. The developer does not run servers and does not receive your game data. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and the optional "remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress, levels, scores, coins, records and collected keepsakes.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, swipe and language.
  • Whether you bought "remove ads", and ad timing (so ads are not shown too often).

This data is kept in the app's local storage and is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "remove ads", the game shows interstitial ads between some levels and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice any time with "Privacy options" in the menu. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

The one-time "remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. Google Play's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: menu → Privacy options (where available).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.
  • Remove all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. Questions: dirtyledsggac@gmail.com
